ORBITWATCH - USER GUIDE
Satellite and space-debris tracking with close-approach alerts
==============================================================================

Open the website at  https://localhost:8443  (start it with start-website.bat).
The first time, the browser warns about the local certificate: choose
Advanced, then Continue to localhost.

Demo logins (password pass1234 for all three; details in pass.txt):
  demo-admin@orbitwatch.test     Administrator
  demo-analyst@orbitwatch.test   Analyst
  demo-viewer@orbitwatch.test    Public Viewer

There are four levels. Each one can do everything the level above it can,
plus its own features:

  1. VISITOR        not logged in
  2. PUBLIC VIEWER  any registered account (new accounts start here)
  3. ANALYST        given by an administrator
  4. ADMINISTRATOR  given by an administrator

The database enforces this too: every level uses its own MySQL account with
only the rights it needs, so even a bug in the website cannot let a visitor
read user accounts or a viewer change the catalogue.


==============================================================================
1. VISITOR (NOT LOGGED IN)
==============================================================================

HOME PAGE
  - A live 3D globe of every tracked object, with the story of the project
    told in four chapters as you scroll.
  - Live counts: objects tracked, close approaches this week.

LIVE GLOBE  (menu: Globe)
  - Every object in Earth orbit as a moving point, positions from SGP4.
  - Search any object by name or NORAD number; click a point for its details,
    its orbit and its next pass.
  - Filters: object type (payload, rocket body, debris), orbital regime
    (LEO, MEO, GEO ...).
  - Layers: close approaches (by risk level), synthetic demo objects, ground
    stations, orbit shells.
  - Maps: original globe, high-resolution imagery, streets, dark, Natural
    Earth (offline); 3D globe or flat 2D map.
  - Time scrubber: move time forward or back and watch the sky change.
  - Replays: watch a close approach, or a simulated avoidance burn, in 3D.
  - Save picture: saves what the globe shows as a PNG.

MISSION DASHBOARD  (menu: Dashboard)
  - Catalogue totals, objects per orbital region (chart), space weather
    (NOAA Kp, F10.7), data freshness, re-entry predictions.
  - Threat radar of the next 72 hours and a countdown to each close approach.
  - The latest AI assessments and system health (scheduler, data sources).
  - "Save image" on the chart and the radar.

CATALOGUE  (menu: Catalogue)
  - Every catalogued object (over 70,000, about 33,000 in orbit), with
    search, filters (type, status, country, owner, orbital region, with or
    without a current orbit) and sorting.
  - Object page: owner, launch, mission, current orbit drawn to scale,
    ground-station passes, its close approaches.

CLOSE APPROACHES  (menu: Close approaches)
  - Every predicted close approach: time of closest approach (TCA), miss
    distance, relative velocity, risk level, probability of collision.
  - Filters: object name, NORAD number, risk level, date range, time windows;
    sort by time or by closest miss.
  - Detail of each event with a 3D replay, and the AI decision support:
    read every assessment, its plain-words explanation, the burn options and
    the full agent trace.
  - Compare two AI assessments side by side and print the comparison.

DEMO LAB  (menu: Demo lab)
  - See synthetic-debris scenarios (clearly labelled SYNTHETIC) and their
    AI assessments.

DATABASE  (menu: Database)
  - The database design: ER diagram, table ring, domains, spotlight,
    treemap and data-flow views, with live row counts.
  - Every table's columns and links; the MongoDB collections.
  - Save any map as SVG or PNG.

HOW IT WORKS  (footer / menu: How it works)
  - The altitude spectrum, the data pipeline and the largest fragmentation
    events, all from live data.

ALWAYS AVAILABLE
  - Search: Ctrl+K or "/" finds objects, pages and glossary terms.
  - Keyboard shortcuts: "?" lists them; "g" then a letter jumps to a page
    (g h home, g g globe, g d dashboard, g c catalogue, g a close
    approaches, g l demo lab, g i how it works).
  - Event log drawer: what OrbitWatch is doing, live (public events).
  - Glossary (help button): every technical term explained; dotted labels
    anywhere open it at that term.
  - Guided tour: a one-minute tour of the interface.
  - System status (top bar): live updates, data freshness, scheduler, space
    weather, Space-Track and e-mail state.
  - A notice at the bottom left when the orbital data is out of date.
  - Create account (top right).


==============================================================================
2. PUBLIC VIEWER (ANY LOGGED-IN ACCOUNT)
==============================================================================

Everything a visitor can do, plus:

FOLLOW SATELLITES
  - "Subscribe to alerts" on any object's page, or type a NORAD number or a
    name on My watchlist / My alerts.

MY WATCHLIST  (menu: Watchlist)
  - Every satellite you follow with a risk timeline of its close
    approaches, from 3, 7 or 14 days back to as far ahead.
  - Dot colour and size = risk; nearby approaches are grouped into one dot
    with a count. Click a dot to open the close approach(es).
  - Totals: approaches ahead, highest risk ahead, closest miss ahead.
  - Refreshes itself when a new screening finishes. Save the timelines as SVG.
  - Unfollow from here.

MY ALERTS  (bell icon, menu: My alerts)
  - An alert for every new close approach of a satellite you follow.
  - Unacknowledged / all; acknowledge one or all; open each in 3D.
  - The bell shows how many are unacknowledged.
  - Your subscriptions, with the next approach of each.

MY ACCOUNT  (click your name -> Account)
  - E-mail alerts on or off, and from which risk level (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH,
    CRITICAL).
  - Notifications on your devices:
      * Turn on for this browser: a pop-up for each new alert at or above
        your chosen risk level, even with OrbitWatch closed (on
        http://localhost or with a trusted certificate; otherwise while an
        OrbitWatch tab is open).
      * The list of your devices with their last delivery and last problem;
        remove any of them.
      * Send a test notification.
  - Change your password (signs out your other sessions).
  - Forgot password: a reset link by e-mail (expires in 30 minutes).
  - Logging out also turns notifications off for that browser.


==============================================================================
3. ANALYST
==============================================================================

Everything a Public Viewer can do, plus:

OBJECT PAGES
  - Altitude history: how the object's orbit has changed (from the MongoDB
    orbit history), with a date range.

REPORTS  (menu: Reports)
  - Objects per orbital region, debris by country, re-entries per year,
    region x year, altitude and decay rate of one object, monthly altitude,
    fastest-decaying objects, re-entry predictions.
  - "Export CSV" on every report; "Save image" on every chart.

EXPORTS
  - CSV of catalogue searches and of close-approach lists.
  - Assessment comparison as CSV or as a PDF file.

AI DECISION SUPPORT  (on a close approach, or in the demo lab)
  - Run AI assessment: the agent checks the encounter (SGP4, probability of
    collision, space weather, ground stations), generates and checks
    avoidance burns, and recommends MONITOR, MANOEUVRE RECOMMENDED,
    NO FEASIBLE MANOEUVRE or DATA UNAVAILABLE.
  - Approve a recommended burn (executed in SIMULATION only: OrbitWatch has
    no command uplink) and watch it on the globe.
  - Reject it with a reason, optionally asking the agent to re-plan with your
    feedback and a minimum miss distance.
  - Compare any two assessments: summary, burn options, agent traces.

DEMO LAB
  - Create a guaranteed close approach with synthetic debris against any
    low-orbit satellite (choose the time, miss distance and crossing angle).
  - Run the AI on it; clear one scenario or all of them.

MORE
  - Optional e-mail when a manoeuvre is approved or rejected (Account page).
  - The event log shows analyst-level events too.


==============================================================================
4. ADMINISTRATOR
==============================================================================

Everything an Analyst can do, plus the Administration page (menu: Admin):

SYSTEM
  - Scheduler state and heartbeat, data sources and their freshness, the
    e-mail queue, the latest reconciliation report.
  - Send yourself a test e-mail (also on the Account page).

USERS & ROLES
  - Search users; create accounts with any role.
  - Change a user's role (Public Viewer / Analyst / Administrator).
  - Deactivate / reactivate or delete accounts.
    (You cannot demote, deactivate or delete yourself.)

REFERENCE DATA
  - Add, edit and delete countries, organisations, launch sites, launch
    vehicles, missions and orbital regions.

WATCHLIST
  - The satellites the screening job checks for close approaches: add or
    remove satellites.

CONFIGURATION
  - Screening threshold (km), horizon, step and maximum element-set age;
    how often data is downloaded; the catalogue, aggregation, re-entry,
    Space-Track and backup schedules; how many backups to keep; after how
    many hours the "data out of date" notice appears.

JOBS
  - Every background job with its last run and result; "Run now" starts one
    (catalogue, ingest, screening, aggregation, re-entry model, backup,
    space weather, e-mail, housekeeping, reconciliation ...).

LOGS
  - Download logs (what was fetched from CelesTrak and Space-Track) and the
    server log files.

DATABASE CLUSTER
  - The MongoDB cluster: every replica-set member and whether it is primary
    or secondary, the shards and how the orbit history is spread over them.

MORE
  - Database error details (table and column names) are shown to
    administrators only.
  - The event log shows administrator events (logins under attack, password
    resets, e-mail failures ...).
  - Repeated failed logins on any account are recorded; the owner is
    e-mailed.


==============================================================================
SECURITY FEATURES EVERYONE BENEFITS FROM
==============================================================================

  - Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes; a password change signs out every
    other session.
  - After 3 wrong passwords on an account (or 5 from one address) the
    browser must pass a short automatic "not a robot" check (about a second,
    nothing to type).
  - Too many failures lock out new browsers for a while; a browser you have
    logged in from before still gets in.
  - After 10 failures the account owner gets a warning e-mail.
  - All state-changing requests need a special header (CSRF protection);
    cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict.


==============================================================================
COMMAND LINE (for whoever runs the server)
==============================================================================

  start-website.bat     start everything (also sets up a new computer)
  ow prepare            apply database updates after pulling new code
  ow mongo-start / ow mongo-stop / ow mongo-status
  ow service            website + scheduler
  ow serve --http --port 5050   plain HTTP for development
  ow create-admin       create an administrator account
  ow ingest / ow screen / ow backup / ow assess EVENT_ID
  See README.md for the full list, ENV_SETUP.md for the settings.
